What to Do if you Suspect Trafficking

Talk to Someone

If there is immediate danger or if you suspect someone is being trafficked, call 911 or your local police service.

In case of an imminent threat or suspicion of human trafficking, please dial 911 or contact your local police service promptly. If you or someone you are aware of requires assistance or you wish to report a possible incident, you can reach out to the Canadian human trafficking hotline at 1-833-900-1010. This hotline operates confidentially, is toll-free, and operates 24/7.

To access additional resources, please refer to Ontario Resources.
